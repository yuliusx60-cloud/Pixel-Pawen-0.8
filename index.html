PIXEL PAWNS

HOW TO PLAY
CHESS
Full rules: castling, en passant, promotion to queen. Tap a piece, then a glowing square.

PAWNS
Each side has N pawns. Move any pawn to any empty square. Win by lining up ALL your pawns after moving each one at least once.

POWERS
FREEZE = you move twice.
SWAP = swap one of your pawns with an enemy pawn.
